# KPI Definitions

## Project

Multi Touch Marketing Attribution and ROI Analytics Dashboard

## Purpose of this Document

This document defines the key performance indicators used in the marketing attribution dashboard. These KPIs help the team measure marketing spend efficiency, channel performance, campaign profitability, conversion impact and revenue contribution.

The KPIs are calculated from ad spend data, customer journey data, conversion data and attribution model outputs.

## 1. Total Spend

### Definition

Total Spend is the total amount of money spent on marketing campaigns across all channels.

### Formula

```text
Total Spend = Sum of all marketing spend
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT SUM(spend) AS total_spend
FROM fact_ad_spend;
```

### Business Meaning

This shows how much the business invested in marketing. It is useful for comparing cost against revenue and deciding whether the advertising budget is being used efficiently.

## 2. Total Revenue

### Definition

Total Revenue is the total amount of money generated from customer conversions.

### Formula

```text
Total Revenue = Sum of conversion revenue
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM fact_conversions;
```

### Business Meaning

This shows the total income generated from customers who completed a conversion. It is one of the main figures used to measure marketing success.

## 3. Total Clicks

### Definition

Total Clicks is the total number of clicks generated by marketing campaigns.

### Formula

```text
Total Clicks = Sum of clicks
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT SUM(clicks) AS total_clicks
FROM fact_ad_spend;
```

### Business Meaning

This shows how many users clicked on marketing campaigns. It is useful for measuring engagement and traffic generation.

## 4. Total Impressions

### Definition

Total Impressions is the total number of times marketing ads were shown to users.

### Formula

```text
Total Impressions = Sum of impressions
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT SUM(impressions) AS total_impressions
FROM fact_ad_spend;
```

### Business Meaning

This shows campaign reach. A high impression count means the advertisement was displayed to many users, but it does not always mean the campaign produced revenue.

## 5. Cost Per Click

### Definition

Cost Per Click measures the average amount spent to generate one click.

### Formula

```text
CPC = Total Spend / Total Clicks
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(spend) / NULLIF(SUM(clicks), 0) AS cpc
FROM fact_ad_spend;
```

### Business Meaning

CPC helps the marketing team understand how expensive it is to bring users to the website or product page. A lower CPC usually means traffic is being acquired more cheaply.

## 6. Cost Per Thousand Impressions

### Definition

Cost Per Thousand Impressions measures the cost of showing an advertisement one thousand times.

### Formula

```text
CPM = (Total Spend / Total Impressions) × 1000
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(spend) / NULLIF(SUM(impressions), 0) * 1000 AS cpm
FROM fact_ad_spend;
```

### Business Meaning

CPM is useful for measuring brand awareness campaigns. It helps compare the cost of reaching audiences across channels.

## 7. Customer Acquisition Cost

### Definition

Customer Acquisition Cost measures the average cost of acquiring one converting customer.

### Formula

```text
CAC = Total Spend / Number of Acquired Customers
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(spend) / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT customer_key), 0) AS cac
FROM fact_ad_spend
CROSS JOIN fact_conversions;
```

### Business Meaning

CAC helps the business know how much it spends to gain one customer. A high CAC may show that a campaign is expensive or inefficient.

## 8. Conversion Rate

### Definition

Conversion Rate measures the percentage of users who converted after interacting with marketing campaigns.

### Formula

```text
Conversion Rate = Number of Conversions / Number of Clicks × 100
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    COUNT(DISTINCT fc.conversion_key)::NUMERIC /
    NULLIF(SUM(fas.clicks), 0) * 100 AS conversion_rate
FROM fact_conversions fc
CROSS JOIN fact_ad_spend fas;
```

### Business Meaning

Conversion Rate shows how effectively campaign traffic becomes revenue. A high conversion rate means the campaign is attracting users who are likely to buy.

## 9. Return on Ad Spend

### Definition

Return on Ad Spend measures how much revenue is generated for every unit of advertising spend.

### Formula

```text
ROAS = Attributed Revenue / Total Spend
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(attributed_revenue) / NULLIF(SUM(total_spend), 0) AS roas
FROM vw_channel_roi_by_model;
```

### Business Meaning

ROAS is one of the most important marketing KPIs. A ROAS of 4 means the business earns 4 units of revenue for every 1 unit spent on advertising.

## 10. Return on Investment

### Definition

Return on Investment measures marketing profit compared to marketing spend.

### Formula

```text
ROI = (Attributed Revenue - Total Spend) / Total Spend × 100
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    (attributed_revenue - total_spend) /
    NULLIF(total_spend, 0) * 100 AS roi_percentage
FROM vw_channel_roi_by_model;
```

### Business Meaning

ROI shows whether a campaign or channel is profitable. Positive ROI means the channel generated more revenue than it cost. Negative ROI means the channel spent more than it returned.

## 11. Revenue Per Customer

### Definition

Revenue Per Customer measures the average revenue generated by each converting customer.

### Formula

```text
Revenue Per Customer = Total Revenue / Number of Customers
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(revenue) / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT customer_key), 0) AS revenue_per_customer
FROM fact_conversions;
```

### Business Meaning

This helps the business understand customer value and compare customer profitability across marketing channels.

## 12. Attribution Credit

### Definition

Attribution Credit is the portion of conversion credit assigned to a marketing touchpoint or channel.

### Formula

```text
Attribution Credit = Model based weight assigned to each touchpoint
```

### Example

If a customer journey has three touchpoints under linear attribution:

```text
Facebook → Google → Email
```

Each touchpoint receives:

```text
1 / 3 = 0.3333
```

### Business Meaning

Attribution credit helps the team understand how much each channel contributed to a conversion.

## 13. Attributed Revenue

### Definition

Attributed Revenue is the portion of conversion revenue assigned to a touchpoint or channel after applying an attribution model.

### Formula

```text
Attributed Revenue = Conversion Revenue × Attribution Weight
```

### SQL Logic

```sql
SELECT
    revenue * attribution_weight AS attributed_revenue
FROM attribution_output;
```

### Business Meaning

Attributed revenue helps the business compare channel value under different attribution models.

## 14. Channel Performance

### Definition

Channel Performance compares marketing channels using spend, clicks, impressions, revenue, CAC, ROAS and ROI.

### Business Meaning

This KPI area helps identify the best and worst performing marketing channels.

## 15. Campaign Performance

### Definition

Campaign Performance evaluates individual campaigns based on cost, engagement, conversion and revenue.

### Business Meaning

This allows the marketing team to decide which campaigns should be scaled, paused or optimized.

## KPI Summary Table

| KPI                  | Formula                           | Purpose                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Total Spend          | Sum of spend                      | Measures marketing cost          |
| Total Revenue        | Sum of revenue                    | Measures income from conversions |
| CPC                  | Spend / Clicks                    | Measures cost of traffic         |
| CPM                  | Spend / Impressions × 1000        | Measures cost of reach           |
| CAC                  | Spend / Customers                 | Measures acquisition cost        |
| Conversion Rate      | Conversions / Clicks × 100        | Measures conversion efficiency   |
| ROAS                 | Revenue / Spend                   | Measures ad spend return         |
| ROI                  | Revenue minus spend / spend × 100 | Measures profitability           |
| Revenue Per Customer | Revenue / Customers               | Measures customer value          |
| Attribution Credit   | Model based weight                | Measures channel contribution    |
| Attributed Revenue   | Revenue × Weight                  | Measures assigned revenue        |

## Dashboard Use

These KPIs are used in the Power BI dashboard to create:

Executive summary cards
Channel comparison charts
Monthly trend charts
Weekly trend charts
Attribution model comparison visuals
ROI and ROAS ranking tables
Campaign performance tables

## Conclusion

The KPI framework helps the project convert raw marketing data into meaningful business insights. These definitions ensure that all team members understand how each metric is calculated and how it should be interpreted in the dashboard.
