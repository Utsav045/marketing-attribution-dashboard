## Dataset Description

The Multi-Touch Marketing Attribution Dataset is designed for marketing attribution analysis and ROI evaluation. The dataset contains information related to marketing spend, customer interactions, and revenue generation across multiple marketing channels.

### 1. Ad Spend Dataset

#### Columns

* Campaign_ID
* Channel
* Spend
* Clicks
* Impressions
* Date

#### Purpose

Used to analyze campaign spending, engagement, and advertising performance.

### 2. Customer Interaction Dataset

#### Columns

* User_ID
* Channel
* Interaction_Date
* Campaign_ID

#### Purpose

Used to track customer journeys and identify marketing touchpoints before conversion.

### 3. Revenue Dataset

#### Columns

* Conversion_ID
* User_ID
* Revenue
* Conversion_Date

#### Purpose

Used to measure campaign effectiveness and revenue contribution.
