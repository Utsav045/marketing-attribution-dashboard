
# Attribution Logic

## First Touch Attribution

Rule:
100% conversion credit assigned to first touchpoint.

Example:

Google Ads → Facebook → Email → Purchase
    
Credit:
Google Ads = 100%

---

## Last Touch Attribution

Rule:
100% conversion credit assigned to last touchpoint.

Example:

Google Ads → Facebook → Email → Purchase

Credit:
Email = 100%

---

## Linear Attribution

Rule:
Credit equally distributed among all touchpoints.

Example:

Google Ads → Facebook → Email → Purchase

Credit:
Google Ads = 33.33%
Facebook = 33.33%
Email = 33.33%
